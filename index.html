Biography

Hi! I am Haocheng Yin, a Ph.D. student in Robotics at Georgia Institute of Technology, where I am fortunate to be co‑advised by Prof. Lu Gan and Prof. Yongsheng Chen. Before joining Georgia Tech, I earned my M.S. in Computer Science at ETH Zürich, during which I had the privilege of completing my Master's thesis at Harvard University under the supervision of Prof. Heng Yang.

My research goal is to develop intelligent embodied agents that are generalizable across diverse tasks and adaptable to unseen environments in the physical world. Toward this goal, my research currently focuses on:

  • Analyzing visual representations for robust robot control.
  • Designing generalizable control modules for sim-to-real transfer.
  • Building compositional generative models for effective world modeling.
